Key Observations
✅ Strong Repeat Engagement
Repeat logins up +13.2% to 309,329 – the standout metric. Bella Italia (+17.8%), Amalfi (+165.8%) and Filling Station (+61.3%) lead. Repeat % now 37.7% of all logins vs 34.3% PY, indicating growing loyalty.
✅ Amalfi & Filling Station Surge
Amalfi logins up +122% (13,138 vs 5,918 PY) – likely new venue openings. Filling Station up +34.6% with Edinburgh High Street as the standout venue (+35.8%). Both brands show strong new user acquisition.
⚠️ Opt-In Rate Declining
Portfolio opt-in rate fell to 72.86% (-4.7pp). Frankie & Benny's dropped -7.8pp to 81.92% and Las Iguanas -7.2pp to 69.27%. Fewer new users are consenting to marketing — review the opt-in journey and consent messaging.
⚠️ New User Acquisition Softening
New users down -1.9% (512,274 vs 521,954 PY). Frankie & Benny's -5.9%, Las Iguanas -2.0%, Bella Italia -1.3%. New marketable users down -6.5% — fewer new guests entering the marketing funnel.
🔴 Café Rouge Significant Decline
Logins collapsed -67.8% (3,397 vs 10,542 PY). Likely reflects estate reduction – multiple venues showing -100% (closed/offline). Remaining venues show reasonable opt-in (66.4%) but dwell time down -14.7%.
🔴 Venue Closures / Offline Venues
Multiple venues reporting 0 logins vs significant PY volumes: F&B Romford, Thanet, Fareham, Falkirk (-100%); Las Iguanas York, Manchester Trafford Centre (-100%); Chiquito Printworks, Chester Broughton (-100%). Requires investigation.

Key Observations
Saturday dominates on volume
Saturday averages 94.6 clicks per day – 3× Friday (54.2) and over 3× the midweek average. All 5 of the top click days are Saturdays. Campaigns served on Saturdays reach the largest active dining audience.
Thursday & Sunday punch above their weight on CTR
Thursday has the highest CTR of any day (1.48%) despite mid-range volume. Sunday also outperforms Friday on CTR (1.45% vs 1.39%), suggesting a more engaged, intent-driven audience on these days.
Friday drives the Fri–Sat–Sun weekend surge
Friday is the second-highest click day (avg 54.2) and acts as the lead-in to Saturday's peak. The Fri–Sat–Sun window accounts for the majority of all campaign clicks, making it the optimal scheduling window.
Tuesday is the weakest day
Tuesday has both the lowest average clicks (30.0) and the lowest CTR (1.25%) – 16% below the portfolio average. Campaigns launched or concentrated on Tuesdays are least likely to drive engagement.
Recommendation for future campaigns: Schedule campaign launches and peak delivery for Thursday–Saturday. If budget is limited, prioritise Saturday for maximum reach and Thursday for maximum conversion efficiency. Avoid Tuesday as a primary launch day.
